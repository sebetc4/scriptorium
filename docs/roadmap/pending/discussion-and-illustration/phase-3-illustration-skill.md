## Objective

Write the `illustration` skill on top of the brick. It says where a figure
goes, how it is composed, how it is proofed without an image entering the
main conversation, and where `diagram-design` takes over.

---

## Overview

### Why This Phase Matters
`diagram-design` draws relations: boxes, arrows, axes, data. It does not draw
a component in cross-section, a doped crystal, a wafer under exposure, an
electronic schematic or a cover. Those figures are drawn today with no rule, and
in radio-fm half of the images carried in the conversation were proofs of them.

### What It Enables
Phase 4's pilot, and every later document whose explanation needs an object
rather than a flow.

### Out of Scope
Interactive figures, and raster images generated by a model.

## Tasks

### The skill
- [ ] Write `.claude/skills/illustration/SKILL.md`: when it fires, the boundary with `diagram-design` (relations there; an object, a material, an electronic schematic or a cover here), what it refuses (raster generation, a fourth font, a colour outside the roles, an interactive figure), and the layout — code in `generators/`, SVG in `document/assets/`
- [ ] Write the composition rules: one idea per figure, text inside a figure on `--muted` and never on `--soft`, a varying phenomenon drawn as small multiples, and a second pass that refines what exists rather than adding to it
- [ ] Write the cover procedure: the illustration is proposed to the user and never decided, per the `pdf` skill's cover rule

- [ ] Route the look at figure proofs through a delegated agent, so that no figure image enters the main conversation

### Constraints
- `diagram-design` is never modified.
- Only one idea is kept from the `canvas-design` skill the user showed:
  refining rather than adding on the second pass. The rest of it contradicts
  the art direction: free fonts, a free palette, a PDF drawn by hand, PNG
  output, content invented for effect.
- The delegate may be `pdf-reviewer` given a figures-only PDF, or a new agent;
  the phase decides, and the report says why.
